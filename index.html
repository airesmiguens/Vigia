05

What this becomes with real telemetry

This build runs entirely on public national statistics and a synthetic station simulation — no distributor data was used or requested to produce it. It exists to show the engine, not to claim access it doesn't have.

Connected to real inputs, the same architecture becomes an operational tool rather than a demonstration:

  • Tank-level feed — from existing automated gauges where installed, or low-cost ultrasonic/float retrofits where they aren't.
  • Predictive stockout modeling — trained on real draw-down and delivery history instead of a synthetic rate.
  • Delivery prioritization — a live, ranked dispatch queue instead of the illustrative one in section 04.
  • Consumer-facing visibility — the same data, surfaced to drivers, reduces the "station-hopping" congestion that costs every station foot traffic and fuel.
